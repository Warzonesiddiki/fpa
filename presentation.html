01 · What is this project?

FP&A from raw data to executive decision

OneFP&A is aimed at FP&A analysts, controllers and CFO teams that need a controlled alternative to disconnected spreadsheets, BI dashboards and cloud EPM workflows.

Local-first

Offline by design

Company data is intended to stay on the user's machine. The product direction excludes cloud sync, telemetry and a hosted web runtime.

Desktop-native

Windows · macOS · Linux

A Tauri desktop shell combines a React webview with a native Rust core and OS services such as the keychain and filesystem.

04 · Industry and enterprise coverage

Generic engines, configurable Industry Packs

Industry behavior is represented as versioned data—not separate industry-specific code. Packs provide COA templates, KPIs, Driver Templates, Report Layouts, calendar presets and GL templates.

Launch Industry Packs

SaaS / Technology · Manufacturing · Retail / CPG · Healthcare · Construction / Engineering · Professional Services · Nonprofit · Government · Energy · Financial Services · Logistics · Real Estate

Pack Builder included in the product scope

Groups and consolidation

Business Units can use different Industry Packs, calendars and currencies. Group reporting covers rollup maps, Intercompany eliminations, Balance Translation, NCI and CTA equity treatment.

Calendar flexibility

Supports 12-month calendars plus 4-5-4, 4-4-5, 5-4-4 and 3-3-3-4 / 13-period patterns, including configurable 52/53-week handling.

05 · Trust and governance

Controls for audit-grade planning

$

Exact money

Rust owns money arithmetic through rust_decimal, integer minor units and decimal strings. Financial paths do not use floating-point values.

✓

Health Check

Checks statement tie-outs, broken references, rounding integrity, missing Driver feeds and anomalies before save or export. Problems are surfaced, not silently fixed.

⌁

Audit Trail

Mutations are designed to produce immutable, HMAC-chained events covering edits, imports, approvals, locks and exports.

🔒

Security at rest

AES-256-GCM protects the Company File; Argon2id protects the PIN; Recovery Phrase and OS Credential Store support local recovery and connector credentials.

↗

Deterministic exports

Excel Export, PDF Export, Model Dump and Auditor Data-Room Export are part of the reporting and governance surface.

A11Y

Accessible screens

Screen State coverage, keyboard workflows, focus management, contrast and axe-based accessibility tests are explicit quality requirements.

07 · Current state and next steps

Ambitious scope, tracked honestly

What is represented in the repository

The repository includes a substantial React/Tauri application structure, 38 MVP feature definitions, 42 tracked screen modules, Rust command registration, Industry Pack data, tests, quality scripts and a large specification suite.

Status is milestone-dependent

What remains before a final release

The taskboard distinguishes DONE, PARTIAL, TODO and BLOCKED work. Native toolchain verification, release CI, signing, performance baselines, full cross-OS E2E and several native integrations remain release considerations.
